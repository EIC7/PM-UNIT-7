Principle of Operation

Figures 1 & 2 illustrate the simple, reliable operating principle of a float level switch. Switching action is obtained through the use of a magnetic sleeve (4) and a float (3), displacer or flow sensing element and a switching mechanism (2).

As level of a liquid in a vessel rises (Figure 1), the float rides on the liquid surface moving the magnetic sleeve upward in the enclosing tube and into the field of the switch mechanism magnet. As a result, the magnet is drawn in tightly to the enclosing tube moving the switch adjusting screw and allowing the activating arm of the snap switch to move, making or breaking the electrical circuit.

Series 8 and F switches are hermetically sealed and designed for high temperature applications up to +400°C (+750°F).
